# SnapParents Monthly Newsletter — Content & Design Guide

## Overview

The SnapParents monthly newsletter is a curated resource for parents and caregivers in tech at Snapchat. Each issue balances practical advice, relevant news, community resources, and upcoming events.

**Audience:** Parents and caregivers navigating parenthood while working in tech
**Frequency:** Monthly (suggested: 1st or 2nd week of each month)
**Format:** HTML email template + optional web archive

---

## Newsletter Structure

### 1. **Header**
- **Logo/Branding:** SnapParents with Snapchat brand colors (yellow)
- **Issue Identifier:** Month and year
- **Tone:** Welcoming and professional

### 2. **Featured Article**
**Purpose:** Highlight one key topic relevant to the community

**Guidelines:**
- Select 1 high-impact article or topic per month
- Include source attribution (publication, author, date)
- Provide a brief intro explaining relevance to parents in tech
- Include 1 actionable key takeaway
- Keep description to 2-3 sentences + takeaway

**Examples:**
- Remote work flexibility policy changes
- Childcare cost trends in tech hubs
- Parental leave best practices
- Work-life balance research
- Mental health resources for working parents

### 3. **Actionable Advice (2-3 topics)**
**Purpose:** Provide practical, immediately useful guidance

**Guidelines:**
- Each advice item should have a clear problem statement
- Provide 2-4 concrete steps or tips
- Always include the source (expert, research, article)
- Keep each item to 150-200 words
- Use formatting (bullet points, numbered lists) for scannability

**Topics to Consider:**
- Time management strategies
- Setting healthy boundaries between work and parenting
- Navigating career growth as a parent
- Building support networks
- Managing parental guilt and imposter syndrome
- Effective communication with managers about parenting needs
- Financial planning for families in tech
- Self-care and burnout prevention

### 4. **News & Trends for Parents in Tech (2-4 items)**
**Purpose:** Keep the community informed about relevant developments

**Guidelines:**
- Source from reputable publications (Wall Street Journal, Harvard Business Review, industry blogs, etc.)
- Include publication name and date
- Focus on news that affects parents specifically:
  - Policy changes (parental leave, workplace flexibility)
  - Industry trends (remote work, cost of living)
  - Research findings (child development, parenting science)
  - Company benefits announcements
  - Economic trends affecting families
- Keep each summary to 1-2 paragraphs
- Explain the "why it matters" angle for parents in tech

### 5. **Resources This Month (5-7 curated items)**
**Purpose:** Provide a targeted toolkit for the community

**Guidelines:**
- Include title, link, and 1-line description
- Categories to include:
  - **Apps/Tools:** Task management, childcare coordination, parental monitoring (age-appropriate)
  - **Articles/Guides:** How-to articles, research summaries
  - **Programs:** Workshops, support groups, mentoring programs
  - **Benefits:** Company perks, external discounts for parents
  - **Organizations:** Nonprofits, support networks (LGBTQ+ parent groups, single parent groups, etc.)

**Curation Tips:**
- Verify links and content still exist
- Include mix of free and premium resources
- Ensure accessibility and inclusivity
- Rotate resources month-to-month to build a comprehensive library over time

### 6. **SnapParents Events**
**Purpose:** Drive engagement and community building

**Guidelines:**
- Structure: Date | Title | Description | CTA Button
- Include:
  - **Date/Time:** Clear format with timezone
  - **Title:** Action-oriented (e.g., "Coffee Chat," "Panel Discussion," "Workshop")
  - **Description:** 1-2 sentences + format (virtual/in-person), speaker/facilitator name if applicable
  - **CTA:** "Learn More / RSVP" linking to event page or Eventbrite
- Event types to include:
  - Monthly virtual coffee chats (low-barrier, recurring)
  - Skill-building workshops (parenting, professional development)
  - Panel discussions (experts sharing insights)
  - Social events (family-friendly, community building)
  - Celebrating milestones (new parent celebrations, work anniversaries)
  - Guest speakers (authors, researchers, company leaders)

**Planning Tip:** Plan events 4-6 weeks in advance to allow community members to schedule

### 7. **Call to Action / Get Involved**
**Purpose:** Encourage community participation

**Guidelines:**
- Invite submissions from the community
- Make it easy: link to email or submission form
- Types of contributions to invite:
  - Articles/resources to feature
  - Event suggestions
  - Advice topics
  - News items
  - Feedback

## Content Best Practices

### Tone & Voice
- **Warm and supportive** without being patronizing
- **Practical and evidence-based** — ground advice in research when possible
- **Inclusive** — acknowledge different family structures, backgrounds, and experiences
- **Actionable** — every section should provide something the reader can do or use

### Inclusivity Checklist
- [ ] Include perspectives from diverse family structures (single parents, same-sex couples, multigenerational households, etc.)
- [ ] Feature parents across different career levels and functions
- [ ] Include resources for parents of children with special needs
- [ ] Highlight international perspectives or remote workers in different time zones
- [ ] Feature content from diverse authors and experts
- [ ] Use inclusive language ("caregivers" not just "mothers/fathers," "partners" not just "spouses")

### Sources & Attribution
- Always credit the original source
- Format: "Source: [Publication Name], [Author], [Date]" or "Source: [Website]"
- Link to original content when possible
- For research: cite reputable institutions (universities, government agencies, established nonprofits)
- For advice: include expert credentials when available

### Research & Verification
- Fact-check all statistics and claims
- Verify links are current and working
- Ensure event details are accurate 48 hours before send
- Have a peer review process before distribution

## Technical Implementation

### Email Rendering
- **Best Practice:** Test in major email clients (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail)
- **Fallback:** Include plain-text version
- **Mobile:** Template is responsive and should render well on phones/tablets
- **Accessibility:** Use semantic HTML, alt text for images, sufficient color contrast

### Distribution
- **Email Service:** Use Snapchat's internal email system or partner with a service (Mailchimp, Constant Contact, etc.)
- **Frequency:** Send on consistent day/time (e.g., 1st Tuesday of month at 9am PT)
- **Subject Line:** Example: "SnapParents [Month]: Work-Life Balance Advice + Upcoming Events"
- **Archive:** Maintain a searchable archive on internal wiki/website

### Templates & Efficiency
- Keep this HTML template in version control
- Create a simple find-and-replace guide for monthly updates
- Consider a publishing workflow: Draft → Review → Approve → Schedule
