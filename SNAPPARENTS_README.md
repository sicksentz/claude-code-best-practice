# SnapParents Monthly Newsletter Design Package

This package contains a complete, reusable design system for SnapParents—Snapchat's Employee Resource Group (ERG) for parents and caregivers—including templates, guidelines, and examples.

## 📋 What's Included

### 1. **snapparents-newsletter-template.html**
The core HTML email template with:
- Responsive design optimized for desktop and mobile
- Snapchat brand colors (yellow/black)
- Six main content sections
- Professional styling and layouts
- Ready to customize with monthly content

### 2. **snapparents-newsletter-guide.md**
Comprehensive content and editorial guidelines covering:
- Newsletter structure and purpose
- Content guidelines for each section
- Inclusivity checklist
- Content calendar template (by month)
- Resource curation tips
- Best practices for tone and sourcing
- Measurement and feedback mechanisms

### 3. **snapparents-newsletter-example.html**
A fully-filled example newsletter (April 2025) showing:
- How to populate the template with real content
- Proper formatting and sourcing
- Event descriptions and CTAs
- Actionable advice structure
- Professional communication style

## 🎯 Core Features

The newsletter is structured around **six key sections**:

1. **Featured Article** — One high-impact piece per month
2. **Actionable Advice (2-3 topics)** — Practical, immediately useful guidance
3. **News & Trends** — Industry-relevant updates for parents in tech
4. **Resources (5-7 items)** — Tools, apps, programs, and support networks
5. **SnapParents Events** — Community gatherings and professional development
6. **Get Involved** — Call-to-action for community submissions

## 🎨 Design Principles

- **Scannable**: Clear headings, visual hierarchy, short paragraphs
- **Inclusive**: Diverse perspectives, multiple family structures, accessibility-first
- **Actionable**: Every section provides something readers can do or use
- **Professional**: Branded, polished, credible sources
- **Flexible**: Easy to customize and evolve based on community feedback

## 🚀 Getting Started

### For Monthly Edition Creation:

1. **Open** `snapparents-newsletter-template.html` in a text editor
2. **Replace** all `[BRACKETED PLACEHOLDERS]` with actual content
3. **Follow** the structure and length guidelines in `snapparents-newsletter-guide.md`
4. **Reference** `snapparents-newsletter-example.html` for tone and formatting
5. **Test** in email clients (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail) before sending
6. **Schedule** for consistent day/time (e.g., 1st Tuesday of month at 9am PT)

### Content Workflow:

- **Week 1 of previous month**: Brainstorm topics and reach out for event submissions
- **Week 2-3**: Source articles, research resources, draft content
- **Week 4**: Internal review and fact-checking
- **Day before send**: Final QA in email clients
- **Send day**: Deploy and monitor engagement

## 📝 Content Guidelines Quick Reference

### Tone
✓ Warm, supportive, and practical  
✓ Evidence-based and research-grounded  
✓ Inclusive and non-judgmental  
✗ Patronizing or preachy  
✗ Overly technical or jargon-heavy  

### Sources
Always include: Author/Publication, Date, Link (when possible)  
Prioritize: Peer-reviewed research, reputable publications, expert credentials  

### Inclusivity Checklist
- [ ] Diverse family structures represented
- [ ] Multiple career levels featured
- [ ] Accessibility considered (alt text, color contrast)
- [ ] Inclusive language ("caregivers," "partners," not gendered assumptions)
- [ ] International/distributed team perspectives

## 📊 Engagement Targets

- **Open Rate**: 30-40%
- **Click-Through Rate**: 10-15%
- **Event RSVP Rate**: 25-35% of subscribers
- **Community Submissions**: 3-5 per month

Monitor these metrics to understand what resonates and iterate.

## 🔧 Technical Notes

### Email Compatibility
- Tested for: Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, mobile clients
- Responsive: Scales from 320px mobile to desktop
- Accessible: Semantic HTML, sufficient color contrast
- Plain-text fallback: Include when distributing via email service

### Customization
All brand colors use Snapchat's signature yellow (`#FFFC00`, `#FFD700`).  
Typography uses system fonts for fast rendering.  
Modify the CSS in `<style>` block to adjust spacing, colors, or fonts.

## 🤝 Community Involvement

Make submission easy for the community:
- **Submit a Resource**: Email snapparents@snapchat.com with title, link, description
- **Suggest a Topic**: Reply to the newsletter or use submission form
- **Event Ideas**: Event lead should share 4-6 weeks in advance
- **Feedback**: Monthly pulse survey on what's working

## 📈 Evolution & Sustainability

**First 3 Months:** Establish baseline, gather feedback, refine based on open rates and comments

**Months 7-12:** Adjust themes based on seasonal needs, archive past issues, plan annual report  
**Ongoing:** Monthly reviews, quarterly feedback sessions, annual refresh of brand/design
